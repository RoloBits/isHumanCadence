  Accepted for publication in ACM Computing Surveys; arXiv v3, April 2025. 37 pages.

## Key claims

- A survey of keystroke-based authentication covering novel keystroke datasets,
  state-of-the-art authentication algorithms, keystroke authentication on touchscreen and
  mobile devices, and applications beyond authentication (abstract).
- Positions keystroke dynamics as a behavioral biometric usable for security,
  verification and identification, and lists future research opportunities (abstract).

## Relevance to this repo

The map of the field, current to 2025. Useful for two jobs: finding datasets beyond Aalto
and CMU when an experiment needs a real-typing corpus, and checking whether a metric idea
(digraph-free timing features, entropy measures, rollover statistics) already has published
results before an experiment re-derives them. The abstract does not say how much space
free-text methods get versus fixed-text — check the full text before leaning on it for
that split.
