# is-human-cadence

Bot detection from typing rhythm. The library observes **when** keys are pressed and released,
never **which** keys, and turns those timings into a score from 0.0 (bot) to 1.0 (human).

Maintained by the `domain-modeling` skill. Glossary only — no weights, no line numbers, no
implementation detail. Those rot; the words should not.

## Capture

**Dwell time**:
How long a single key is held — its release minus its own press.
_Avoid_: hold time, press duration

**Flight time**:
The gap between one key being released and the next being pressed. Recorded only when exactly one
key is down, so rollover does not contaminate it.
_Avoid_: inter-key interval, IKI, gap, latency

**Rollover**:
A key pressed while another is still down. Common in fast human typing, rare in naive automation.
_Avoid_: overlap, chord

**Correction**:
A Backspace or Delete press. Counted as an event; the key identity is compared and discarded, never
stored.
_Avoid_: typo, erase, undo

**Synthetic event**:
A keyboard event the browser reports as not user-generated. The library counts these but does not
treat the count as proof on its own.
_Avoid_: fake, injected, bot event

**Window**:
The most recent N dwells and N flights, held in a fixed-size buffer. Older timings fall out; the
score always describes recent typing, not the whole session.
_Avoid_: history, buffer, sample set

## Scoring

**Metric**:
One named signal derived from the window, normalised so that higher always means more human. Six
of them exist.
_Avoid_: feature, factor, dimension

**Gating**:
Dropping a metric that has no signal to speak of out of the weighted mean entirely — out of both
the numerator and the denominator, so the remaining weights redistribute. Absence of a signal is
not evidence of a bot.
_Avoid_: skipping, zeroing, masking

**Spoof detection**:
The statistical test asking whether flight times look like they were drawn from a real human
distribution or generated by a program. It is the largest single input to the score.
_Avoid_: anti-bot check, validation, verification

**Burst**:
A run of fast typing bounded by a long pause. Humans type in bursts and pause to think; the
regularity of the pauses is itself a signal.
_Avoid_: chunk, streak, session

## Output

**Score**:
The weighted mean of the surviving metrics, from 0.0 to 1.0. Higher is more human.
_Avoid_: confidence, probability, rating

**Confident**:
Whether enough samples have been collected to report a score at all. It is a **sample count
threshold and nothing more** — it says nothing about how reliable the score is. This is the most
misread word in the library.
_Avoid_: certain, reliable, significant, confidence

**Classification**:
The three-state verdict — bot, unknown, human — derived from the score with a dead zone in each
direction so it does not flicker on a borderline typist. It is computed **independently of
`confident`**, so a verdict can be emitted before there are enough samples to trust it.
_Avoid_: verdict, label, result, decision

**Signal**:
A flag or count describing something noticed about the input rather than scored from it — a paste,
synthetic events, too little data, text arriving without keystrokes. A signal is reported alongside
the score and is never folded into it. Their stated purpose is to let a caller tell a bot apart
from **assistive technology**: dictation and autofill produce text with no keystrokes at all, which
is not a bot and must not be scored as one.
_Avoid_: flag, warning, indicator
